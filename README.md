# Features
26 Jokers (11 Uncommon, 5 Rare, 9 Legendary)

1 Tarot Card

2 Secret Spectral Cards

16 Class Cards, a new, pretty strong consumable type that can provide significant benefits - if the numbers are in your favor! With it comes 4 types of Schedule Pack; 2 normal, 1 Jumbo, 1 Mega.

1 Deck, that comes with its own Sleeve if you have CardSleeves installed!

1 Enhancement

1 Seal

# CONSUMABLES

## Class Cards

<img width="1704" height="570" alt="Class Cards" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cfd59413-aba1-4024-8277-3d29dcfab355" />

Class Cards are a brand new set of sixteen consumable cards, most of them having some kind of familial ties to The Wheel of Fortune. By that, I mean most of them will only trigger their effect on random chance (a 1 in 2 chance, specifically), and if it doesn't trigger? Well, you'll just be met with a <img width="64" height="64" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8b73041-84b5-40de-8eba-7b0df91c95c1" />


Here they all are in order:

**000. The Halls**: One of the only two that aren't random, this one spawns a random Student joker into your slots (from earlier, that's any of the Jokers in images 1-4, and Chip)

**001. Mathematics**: When the chance succeeds, this card will tack on a +5 Mult bonus to two selected cards. This bonus is permanent, like the Chips bonus the Hiker joker from base-game Balatro gives to played cards.

**002. Language Arts**: If the dice roll right, this card will add random Enhancements to up to 3 selected cards. These enhancements can be anything such as Gold, Steel, Glass, Lucky, Wild, etc.

**003. Science**: When it feels like it, this class will level up your most played hand of the run by three levels. So, it's kind of like the Orbital Tag from the base game, except I have no clue how the Orbital tag selects its target hand. It might be the most played hand? I don't know. This class does not synergize well with Obelisk and The Ox.

**004. History**: When the numbers align, this card will give the player 2 extra hands. It's part of a trio with the next two in that they give +2 things related to card selection.

**005. Art**: Basically History, but instead of +2 hands, you get +2 Hand Size. (The default is 8, with one trigger of Art it goes up to 10 cards in your hand.)

**006. Music**: The last member of the "Cultural Arts Trio", this one gives +2 Discards, just like the Joker of the man who teaches that class.

**007. Geometry**: Kind of a stretch to include this one, but something Compass-related had to synergize with Circle's class card. This one is more directly related to Hiker in that they both give Chips bonuses; this class card gives +25 Chips on successful trigger.

**008. Theatre**: For the next 4 there wasn't a representative teacher so I just went wild with the art. What's also wild is this lil' number's effect when the wheel chooses fortune; this card adds Negative to one of your jokers, with no hand size penalty unlike the base-game Spectral card Ectoplasm.

**009. P.E.**: In earlier closed builds, this card once shaved off 5% of the blind requirement score, but it generally not working as intended and a want to complement the next card up led me to overhaul it. Now, it has a chance to give an extra card slot in the Shop, like the Overstock family of vouchers.

**010. Swimming**: This was the card I said I wanted to complement with the last card; this one will give an extra booster pack slot in the shop. Yep, that's three things Zip-related that deal with booster packs. They're like treasure. Which pirates **love**.

**011. Chorus**: This card's art is meant to be an homage to [the video that started everything with this fandom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V88_lZRwP70), and [the song it was a fan-made music video of.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=weZKm1kTrpc) Will we finally be free from this cage? Probably not, so just keep behaving and making [NUMBER GO UP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXZX_HAmbTU). And if it wants to, this card can help do exactly that, giving to two selected cards a random Edition, such as Foil, Holographic, or Polychrome. Or maybe even Negative.

**012. Computer Lab**: A pretty direct reference to the non-FPE Katieverse (which I MAY make some jokers of in a future update), this card, when the roulette runs in your favor, will spawn a random Rare joker. This is like the Spectral card Wraith, but unlike Wraith, this card won't take your money (or give some to you if you happen to be in debt). Agetha doesn't care about your money.

**013. Detention**: No, don't worry, you ain't getting murdered. This card, when the dice succeed, will just hold you back an Ante, like the Heiroglyph/Petroglyph vouchers. This could give you more time to better your joker setup for the tests soon ahead.

**014. Administration**: The other non-random one, this one spawns up to two of its subordinates in your consumable slots, granted you have room.

**015. Examination**: Pass the test by praying to Miss Fortune, and you'll find a nice A+ Seal on one selected card. When played and scored, this seal will level up the played poker hand it's a part of.

## Other Consumables (And The Modifiers)

<img width="1000" height="600" alt="Other Consumables and Modifiers" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7ccaabf2-f928-4016-bbae-8e6b08000533" />

**The Notebook**: (Tarot) Applies the Paper enhancement to 1 selected card.

**Teacher's Lounge**: (Spectral) 0.3% chance to replace any Class Card in a Schedule Pack (the booster packs for the Class Card set) or any Spectral Card in a Spectral Pack. Kind of like the Soul. On use, it spawns one of the Teacher jokers (on the last post, it would be the jokers in images 5 and 6.)

**???**: (Spectral) Same spawning conditions as the Teacher's Lounge, but I'm warning you, do not go in there. Some say it awakens a great evil laying at rest inside that room. Actually, you know what? No point being coy, this card spawns a copy of Alice in your Jokers.

**Paper**: (Enhancement) Obtained from random enhancement granters or The Notebook, cards with this Enhancement give 1.5x Chips, but have a 1 in 4 chance of being ripped up after the played hand.

**A+ Seal**: (Seal) This seal, applied to a card from a random seal granter or a successful Examination, will level up the played poker hand it's a part of whenever it scores. This includes getting retriggered by jokers like Edward (if it has an Enhancement) or the base game's Seltzer.

## Boosters

The new Booster Packs for this mod are called Schedule Packs! They cost $2 more than their counterparts due to their contents' power, and contain the same amount of Class Cards as an Arcana/Celestial pack contains Tarots/Planets. That is:

3, pick 1 on the standard Packs\
5, pick 1 on the Jumbo Pack\
5, pick 2 on the Mega Pack
